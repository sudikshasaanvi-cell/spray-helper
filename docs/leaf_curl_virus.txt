Leaf curl  :
Tomato leaf curl virus
Field Diagnostic Symptoms
Severe stunting of plants
Downward rolling and crinkling of the leaves
Older leaves become leathery and brittle
Shortening of internodes
Appearence of more lateral branches - bushy appearance
Infected plant
Downward curling of leaf
Causal organism -
Tomato leaf curl virus (TLCV)
Vector :
Whiteflies –Bemisia tabaci
Survival and Mode of Spread
Primary spread:
Whiteflies.
Secondary spread:
Movement of infected plants
Integrated Disease Management
Raising nursery in protected condition
Growing Sorghum, Maize or Bajra as barrier crop
Use of disease free seeds
Yellow sticky traps @ 5nos./ac
Remove alternate weed host - Abutilon indicum
Spraying Dimethoate 30 EC @ 200 ml/ac or Methyl demeton 25 EC @ 200 ml/ac or Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 200 g/ac or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 200 ml/ac
